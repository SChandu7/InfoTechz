Privacy Policy

MediTime by INFOTECHZ SOFTWARE SERVICES · Last updated: 25 October 2026

MediTime is a pharmacy counter service. A pharmacy uploads a patient's prescription and medicine photos, a pharmacist checks the result, and the patient receives a medicine schedule (image and short video) on WhatsApp. This page explains what data we use and how we protect it.

1. Who we are

INFOTECHZ SOFTWARE SERVICES, a Udyam-registered sole proprietorship, Guntur, Andhra Pradesh, India.
Contact: dev@chandus7.in

2. What data we collect

DataWhy we use it
Patient name and WhatsApp numberTo send the medicine schedule to the right person
Prescription photoTo read the medicines, doses and timings
Medicine pack / strip photosTo match each medicine and show its picture in the schedule
Message status (sent, delivered, read)To let the pharmacy know the patient received the schedule
Pharmacy staff login detailsTo let authorised staff use the system

We do not collect payment card details, and we do not track patients' location.

3. How we use it

Only to create and deliver the medicine schedule, and to let the pharmacy see and correct it. We do not use patient data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

4. Who we share it with

ServiceWhat it does
Google (Gemini API)Reads the prescription and medicine photos
Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform)Delivers the schedule to the patient's WhatsApp
Amazon Web Services / cloud hostingStores data securely on our servers
Microsoft (text-to-speech)Creates the voice narration for the video (text only, no photos)

We share data only with these service providers, only for the purposes above, or when required by law.

5. How long we keep it

Prescription photos, medicine photos and generated schedules are deleted automatically after 90 days. A pharmacy can ask us to delete a record earlier.

6. How we protect it

Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. Only logged-in pharmacy staff can see their own shop's records. Access to our servers is restricted.

7. Your choices

You can stop receiving messages at any time by replying STOP on WhatsApp or by telling the pharmacy. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data.

8. Data deletion

To delete your data, email dev@chandus7.in with your WhatsApp number and the pharmacy name, or ask the pharmacy directly. We delete it within 7 working days and confirm by email or WhatsApp.

9. Children

A schedule for a child is requested by a parent or guardian at the pharmacy and is sent to the number they provide.

10. Medical note

MediTime helps patients follow their prescription. It is not a substitute for advice from a doctor or pharmacist. Every schedule is checked by the pharmacy before it is sent.

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
